QEC Lake makes heterogeneous quantum error-correction datasets searchable and reusable for
AI decoder training and benchmarking. It combines experimental-data ingestion, simulation,
synthetic data generation, metadata management, and model-specific data preparation.

Published as a demo paper in **PVLDB 19(12), pages 4574–4577 (2026)**.
