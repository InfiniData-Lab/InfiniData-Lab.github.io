Infinidata is happy to announce that we will show our work on Data Management for Quantum Error Correction and Quantum Circuit Simulation at this year's Delft Quantum Showcase. 

We will present our recent works on QEC Lake and QEC Model Zoo as well as the works on Qymera and QuWARP. 

Visit us to see the fusion of Data Management and Quantum Computing and its innovation to drive future research in the field.
